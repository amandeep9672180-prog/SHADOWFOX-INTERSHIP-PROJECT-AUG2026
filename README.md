# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis – ShadowFox Internship

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as **Task 1 of my ShadowFox Data Analytics Internship**. The objective was to analyze Superstore sales data using **Microsoft Excel** and convert raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

The project involved data cleaning, preparation, analysis, visualization, and dashboard creation. I analyzed key business metrics such as **Sales, Profit, Orders, Customers, and Quantity**, along with category-wise and region-wise performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Understand order and customer activity.
* Compare performance across different product categories.
* Analyze regional sales and profitability.
* Identify important trends and patterns in the data.
* Create meaningful KPIs for quick performance monitoring.
* Build an interactive Excel dashboard using charts and slicers.
* Convert data analysis into useful business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel**
* PivotTables
* PivotCharts
* Excel Formulas
* KPI Cards
* Slicers
* Data Cleaning & Preparation
* Data Visualization

## 🔄 Project Workflow

**Raw Dataset → Data Cleaning → Data Preparation → Analysis → PivotTables → Charts → KPIs → Slicers → Interactive Dashboard → Business Insights**

## 📈 Key Analysis

### Sales Analysis

Analyzed overall sales performance and compared sales across different categories and regions.

### Profit Analysis

Analyzed profitability to understand which categories and regions contributed more to overall profit.

### Category Analysis

Compared product categories based on sales, profit, and quantity to identify strong and weak-performing categories.

### Regional Analysis

Compared regional performance to identify regions with higher and lower sales and profitability.

### Order & Customer Analysis

Analyzed order volume and customer activity to understand overall business performance.

## 📊 Dashboard

The final Excel dashboard contains:

* KPI Cards
* Sales Analysis Charts
* Profit Analysis Charts
* Category Performance
* Regional Performance
* Order & Customer Metrics
* Interactive Slicers

The dashboard was designed to provide a quick and clear overview of business performance and make the analysis easier to explore.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

* High-performing and low-performing categories.
* Regions contributing strongly to sales and profit.
* Differences between sales performance and profitability.
* Overall order and customer activity.
* Trends and patterns that can support business decision-making.
